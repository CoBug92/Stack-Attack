Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

Stack Attack is an offline game. We respect your privacy and do not collect personal data.

Data processed by the app

The app saves your high score, selected visual settings, and haptics preference on your device. This information is used only to operate the game, does not identify you, and is not sent to the developer.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not create or require an account.
  • We do not collect personal information, usage data, identifiers, or location data.
  • We do not use advertising, third-party analytics, or trackers.
  • We do not share or sell data to third parties.

Children

Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that requires data processing, we will update this page and its effective date before releasing that version.
